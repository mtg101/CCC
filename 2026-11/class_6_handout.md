# Introduction to Block Code
A 6-week course for students new to making computers do what they're told. 

## Class 6 - December 12th

### Title: Happy Holidays!
Let's make our own animated scenes in Scratch showing how we all celebrate winter in different ways, and then share them with the class. 

### Course: Start from blank project at https://scratch.mit.edu/

### Goal: Make an animated winter celebration scene
- start with a blank new project and create a winter celebration scene
- start with a background, then add sprites for people and objects, probably some food :)

### How to ask for help
If you get stuck ask for help - and when you ask it helps if you tell us what you're trying to do, and what isn't working, like 'I've added sprites for my friends but they are the wrong colour' rather than just shouting 'it's broken it's broken'.

### Stretch Goals: 
- make the scene interactive
    - can you change the colour of lights by clicking them?
    - can people in the scene say things when you click on them?
- play a winter tune
    - what tune makes you think of winter?
    - how can you play that tune?
- check out other students scenes and learn about how they celebrate winter
    - and talk to your own family about the ways that other people celebrate
    - do you have any special family celebrations that have been passed down?
